# Legacy QR checkout

The static SBI QR/manual-confirmation flow has been replaced for storefront orders by Razorpay hosted checkout. See [Razorpay checkout setup](razorpay-checkout.md) for gateway credentials, the signed `payment_link.paid` webhook, and the retained ₹10 online-order limit.

The SBI QR remains a separate payment asset and is not used by storefront Online checkout or as a payment confirmation source.
